    Which bin does it go in?

      Getting it right

      • Have everything out by 6am on collection day, at the front edge of your property, close to the pavement but not on it.
      • Flatten cardboard boxes before they go in the blue bin or box.
      • Empty and rinse containers before they go in the green bin or box.
      • Peel film lids off pots, tubs and trays. The film goes in general waste.
      • No food in the general waste bin. Food goes in the caddy, which is collected every week.
      • If a collection is missed, leave your bins out and check the service status at croydon.gov.uk/bin-status.
